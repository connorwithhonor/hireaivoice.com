You do not have a marketing problem. You are already getting calls. You have an answering
problem.

## AD HOOKS
First 3 seconds decide everything. Test all 4 as separate creatives.

1. "You missed a call yesterday. That caller booked somebody else."
2. "Your phone is your best salesperson and it goes to voicemail 30 percent of the time."
3. "Call 661-299-7299 right now. That is an A I. Ask it anything."
4. "One booked job a month pays for this. The agent never calls out."

Hook 3 is the strongest and the riskiest. It sends traffic to a phone number instead of a
landing page, so it is harder to attribute, but it is the only hook where the prospect
experiences the product before they ever see a price. Track it with a dedicated number if
you want clean numbers.

## WHAT NOT TO DO
Taken straight from the ad we tore apart this morning, so we do not become it.

- No income screenshots. No "cash collected" figures. Ever.
- No fake scarcity, no countdown timers, no "only 3 spots."
- No claims you cannot verify from your own logs.
- Do not promise a number of jobs. Promise answered calls, which is the thing you control.
- No AI-generated faces and no stock people anywhere in the creative.
