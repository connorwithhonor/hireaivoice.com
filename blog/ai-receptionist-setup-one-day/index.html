The Short Version

  • AI receptionist setup includes a carrier check, approved business information, and tests of the actual call flow before launch.
  • We build your agent from that conversation, then test it against real call scenarios before it ever touches your line.
  • Your team answers first. The AI takes over after hours, on closed days, and when the line is busy.
  • The total effort on your side is one short conversation and a quick review. That is it.

How Do You Set Up an AI Receptionist?

Connecting an AI receptionist starts with an agreed call flow and a check of your existing phone setup. Before that call, you provide what you do, your approved pricing answers, common questions, service area, hours, and booking rules. We configure and test the agent, then use the short call to set conditional forwarding on your existing line.

I have spent 27 years in Santa Clarita real estate, and before that I wore a badge with the LAPD and worked corrections. I have sat across from a lot of busy people who assumed any new system meant weeks of headaches. This is the opposite. You answer the questions you already know the answers to, and you go back to work. We do the building.

What Happens Before the 15-Minute Setup Call?

We collect what your AI receptionist needs to represent your business. You provide five things:

1. Your services. What you do and what you do not do, so the agent never books a job you do not take.

2. Your pricing. Set prices, ranges, trip fees, or "we quote on site," whatever is true. The agent uses only the answers you approve.

3. Your FAQs. The questions you answer ten times a day. Hours, service area, do you offer free estimates, how soon can you come out. We load the real answers.

4. Your booking process. How you want appointments set, what you need from the caller, and how it lands in front of you so you just show up to the job.

5. Your voice. How you greet people, the name they should hear, and the tone that fits your business.

We use those answers to build the approved call flow before the forwarding call.

What Do I Need to Provide?

Only the answers you already carry in your head. There are no scripts to write, no software to learn, and no equipment to buy. You do not need a tech person. If you can describe your business to a new employee on their first day, you can set up an AI receptionist, because that is exactly what the onboarding call is.

You keep your number, your provider, and control of the forwarding.

The AI receptionist works with your existing line, so nothing about your truck wraps, your business cards, your listings, or your ads has to change. Your customers call the number they already know. Your team answers first.

How Is the Agent Built and Tested?

After the call, we build your agent and run it through real call scenarios before it ever touches your line. This is the part owners do not see, and it is where the quality comes from. We do not hand you a raw bot and wish you luck. We pressure-test it first.

We test price shoppers, emergencies, callers who ramble, and questions asked several ways. We confirm the agent follows the approved answers, qualification steps, appointment rules, and handoff process. Then you review it before a real customer reaches it. If something is off, we adjust it.

How Long Does the Connection Take?

The setup call is scheduled after the scope and carrier requirements are confirmed. We set conditional forwarding on your existing number. Your team keeps answering first. The AI takes over after hours, on closed days, and when the line is busy.
