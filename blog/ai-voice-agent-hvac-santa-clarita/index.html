How Does the AI Triage an Emergency Versus Routine Call?

The AI asks approved questions and sorts each call it handles by urgency. A unit that is completely down during a heat wave, a home with seniors or infants, a no-cool with a baby inside, or a burning smell can be flagged for same-day or after-hours review.

For a filter swap, seasonal tune-up, or quote request, the agent can offer an available routine service time. It collects the address, unit symptoms, and urgency using your approved call flow.

That triage gives your AI receptionist for missed calls an approved way to separate urgent requests from routine ones.
