What Does the Starter Plan Include?

Starter at $397/month is the core engine: a 24/7 AI voice agent for the calls your team misses. Your team gets first shot at the phone. The AI answers after hours, on closed days, and when the line is busy. It says it is an AI in the first breath, follows your approved call flow, and offers available times.

Use your own call log, callback results, and completed work to measure the gap. Do not count each missed call as a sale. See how to estimate missed-call opportunity.

And the phone is only the part you can hear. Starter also opens the whole platform to you, at no extra charge, because it costs me nothing to hand you the keys:

  • A real CRM with unlimited contacts. Caller records, conversations, and jobs stay in one place instead of three notebooks and a phone.
  • Unlimited users. Your office manager, your techs, your bookkeeper, your spouse. Everybody gets their own login. There is no per-seat charge, ever.
  • Online booking that syncs to your calendar and checks it for conflicts, so you cannot get double-booked.
  • Review requests that go out automatically after a finished job.
  • A social media planner, so a month of posts takes one sitting.
  • Forms, pipelines, contracts with e-signature, and invoices.

You do not have to use any of it. It is there because you are paying for the system, not for a single feature. A basic answering bot handles the phone. This system also includes front-office tools.

What Does the Full System Plan Add?

Full System at $597/month is not more software. It is me running the software for you. Starter gives your team access to the platform. Full System adds Connor's implementation and ongoing operation.

On Full System I build it and I keep it running:

  • Your pipeline, set up and maintained. I build the stages around how your jobs actually move, and keep the CRM clean instead of letting it rot into a contact dump.
  • Nurture sequences I write and manage. The people who called and did not book get texted and emailed on a schedule, so they do not go cold and end up calling somebody else in three weeks.
  • Review requests I monitor. Not just fired automatically, watched. When one does not land, or a bad one shows up, you hear about it from me.
  • Your social posts scheduled. You send me what happened this month, I put it on the calendar.
  • Listings management across the directories. Your name, address and phone are maintained across supported directories.

The two hundred dollar difference is my hours, plus the listings bill. Not a more expensive version of the same product. If you have somebody in your office who will actually run the platform, stay on Starter and keep the two hundred. If you do not, and you were never going to, Full System is me being that person.

Starter answers the phone. Full System means somebody is minding the whole front office, and it is me.
