How Much Does One Missed Call Actually Cost?

You cannot know the value of one missed call from the call log alone. The caller may have become a job, may have called back, or may not have been a fit. Measure the opportunity with your own call and sales data.

How Do You Calculate the Annual Leak?

Multiply missed calls per week by your estimated conversion rate, average job value, and 52 weeks. Treat the result as an estimate. Then compare it with actual outcomes from returned and covered calls.

Why Do Missed Calls Cost More Than They Look?
