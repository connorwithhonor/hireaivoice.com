What Is Speed to Lead?

Speed to lead is how fast your business responds to a new inquiry, measured from the moment a call, form, or text comes in to the moment a real conversation starts. Track it next to the contact result and booked work.

For a service business in Santa Clarita or Los Angeles County, a fast answer matters. A caller with an active problem may keep dialing until somebody starts helping.

How Fast Should You Respond to a New Lead?

Set a target your team can measure and meet. Five minutes can be a practical internal goal for an urgent inbound request. Track contact and booking results with your own data.

Why Service Businesses Miss the First Call

It is not laziness. It is the nature of the work. The plumber is under a sink. The HVAC tech is on a roof in 100-degree heat. The roofer is on a ladder. The agent is mid-showing. You physically cannot pick up each call live, and the calls do not wait for a convenient time. They come in while you are doing the exact work that earns the money.

The call log shows the delay. The callback result shows the outcome.

When a call rolls to voicemail, the caller may hang up and try another business. You may never know the call came in. Track that gap before spending more to make the phone ring.

How an AI Voice Agent Covers the Gap

An AI voice agent covers calls your team misses after hours, on closed days, and when the line is busy. Your team gets first shot at the phone. The AI says it is an AI in the first breath, greets the caller by your business name, follows approved answers, qualifies what they need, and can offer a booking.

This is the point of an AI receptionist for missed calls. A customer with a burst pipe at 9 PM can reach the AI, share the job details, and hear available appointment times instead of reaching voicemail.

Missed-Call Text-Back: The Safety Net

Missed-call text-back gives the caller another path. If a call is not answered live, the system can send a text: "Sorry we missed you, this is [your business], how can we help?"

What to Do This Week

You do not need a new marketing budget to fix this. You need to stop leaking the leads you already paid for. Three moves:

1. Measure it. For one week, track calls answered live, calls that roll over, callback results, and booked work.

2. Put a 24/7 backstop in place. An AI voice agent can answer and offer a booking when your team misses the call.

3. Add text-back, so a caller has another way to reach your team.
