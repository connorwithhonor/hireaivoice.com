How call forwarding works, by carrier

You keep your number. You keep your provider. You set your line to roll to us only when nobody picks up or the line is busy. It takes about five minutes, you do it yourself, and you can undo it in thirty seconds.

You never set a schedule. Forwarding on no-answer does not care what time it is. It cares whether a human picked up.

The part most people get wrong

Do not use "forward all calls"

That sends every call to us, including the ones your front desk would have answered fine. It is the setting that makes owners nervous, and they are right to be.

Use "forward on no answer"

Your desk rings first, every time. Only after four or five rings, when nobody has picked up, does the call roll to us instead of to voicemail.

Add "forward on busy"

Catches the calls that arrive while your line is already tied up. Those are invisible today. They do not ring, they do not leave voicemail, they just go somewhere else.

Why this means you never schedule anything.

Staff at lunch from twelve to one? The phone rings, nobody picks up, we answer.

Closed at five? Same thing.

Someone in the back and the line is busy at 2:30 on a Tuesday? Same thing.

Snow day and nobody came in? Same thing.

Set it once and never touch it again. No star codes twice a day, and nobody has to remember to switch it back at one o'clock. That last one matters, because the day somebody forgets is the day the assistant answers all afternoon.

Common questions

Do my customers ever see a different number?

No. They dial the number they always dialed. Our number is never shown to them and never given out.

Can I turn it off myself?

Yes, from your own phone, in about thirty seconds, without calling us first. The codes are in the last column above.

What if I want it off for a week?

Turn it off. Turn it back on when you want it. There is nothing on our end you need to coordinate.

Do I have to move my number to you?

No, and we will not offer to. Moving a business number takes days and can knock out your fax and your card terminal. Forwarding is reversible in seconds. We do not touch your number.

How many rings before it forwards?

Four or five is typical, which is fifteen to twenty seconds. On most VoIP systems you can set it exactly. On a cell it is usually fixed by the carrier.

What if I already forward to an answering service?

Tell us. A line can generally only forward to one place, so we sort out the order before you change anything. Nothing breaks by accident.
