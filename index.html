Before you put AI on your phone.

Will it replace my team or change my number?

The starting setup covers agreed after-hours or overflow calls. Your carrier must support the forwarding path. Your team and existing number stay central to the process.

What if it gets something wrong?

AI can make mistakes. We test the approved call flow, limit what it is allowed to answer, and define the human handoff and fallback before launch. It is not an emergency service.

Does it confirm appointments?

It can capture a preferred time. A confirmed booking requires a supported, connected calendar that has passed conflict, availability, and failure testing. We specify which behavior you are buying.

Are calls recorded or transcribed?

Audio recording, AI processing, written transcripts, and stored summaries are separate settings. We explain the actual configuration, notice, consent, and retention before activation. Switching off audio recording does not mean no call data is processed.

Can I add the rest of HonorElevate later?

Yes. We scope the next useful workflow after reviewing the first one. Platform access does not mean every feature is configured or that ongoing custom work is unlimited.

What happens when the trial ends?

The written offer and checkout must state the dates, any automatic billing, amount, usage terms, and cancellation method. Confirm them before starting. Turning off call forwarding does not itself cancel a subscription.
