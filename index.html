Questions

I already have a phone number. Do I have to change it?

No, and you never will. You keep your number and your provider. We set your line to forward to us only when nobody answers or the line is busy. Your customers dial exactly what they always dialed.

Is this going to replace my front desk?

No. It answers when your team can't: after close, on days you're shut, and when the line is already tied up. Your front desk gets a summary in the morning, not a competitor.

What if it says something wrong to my customer?

It's hard-walled. No prices beyond what you approve in writing, no advice, no guessing. Clinical or legal questions get one answer: that's for the doctor, or that's what the consultation is for. Call the demo line and try to make it break that rule.

Are calls recorded?

No. Recording is off by default. California requires all-party consent, so we removed the requirement instead of managing it.

Does it book straight into my scheduling software?

It captures the appointment and texts your front desk within a minute. If you use Google Calendar we can book directly into it. Writing into a practice management system like Dentrix is a bigger project and we won't promise it on day one.

How long does setup take?

One form and one short call. The forwarding itself takes about five minutes on your end and you can switch it off yourself whenever you want.
