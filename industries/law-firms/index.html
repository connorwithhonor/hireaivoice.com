What must an AI legal receptionist never do?

I will not have the AI give legal advice, interpret documents, assess a case, calculate a deadline, or recommend action. It cannot quote fees, predict outcomes, promise representation, or dismiss a request as unimportant. Medical and insurance advice stay out, too. I keep the receptionist inside its administrative job.

When a caller reports an urgent deadline, I preserve it as reported and use your approved human escalation. The AI does not validate the date or imply waiting for a callback preserves rights. Your attorneys approve that wording and determine which urgent situations the firm can actually handle.

I treat emergency escalation as a reviewed contact process. I need the recipient, staffed hours, and fallback when they cannot be reached. It is not a guarantee an attorney answers immediately. HireAIVoice is not an emergency service, and I will not let automated intake sound like active legal assistance.
