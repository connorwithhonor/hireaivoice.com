I ask for a name, callback number, general service interest, and preferred appointment window. Then I distinguish a new consultation from a call about an existing visit. I want that answer early. A concern after treatment must not end up on your desk labeled as another sales lead.

For med spa intake, I collect only what lets your desk take the next action. A general inquiry does not call for an extensive medical history. When a clinical question comes up, I use your approved route to staff. Your clinical team decides what the later assessment needs, not a marketing questionnaire.

I let callers correct details or withhold them. The record must separate what was said from what the AI could not obtain. What must the receptionist never say about treatment?

I do not let the receptionist diagnose, select procedures, recommend medication, quote treatments, or give medical advice. I also keep insurance advice outside this call flow. No interpreting benefits, promising reimbursement, or deciding coverage. I can preserve the caller's insurance question for staff. Any administrative facts the AI shares need your approval, and I do not let those facts turn into advice for that individual's treatment.
