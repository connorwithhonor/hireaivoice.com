Agree on the rules before launch.

  • AI identification at the beginning of the call.
  • Approved knowledge and a clear path to a person.
  • Actual recording, transcription, consent, and retention settings.
  • Alert delivery checks, a backup route, and a named responder.
  • Usage budget, support scope, and cancellation steps.

SMS and email follow-up require the appropriate permissions and channel configuration. Emergency response, professional advice, and custom software integrations are not assumed parts of a routine phone pilot.
